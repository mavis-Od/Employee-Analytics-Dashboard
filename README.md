# Employee Analytics Dashboard | Power BI

## Project Overview

This project presents an interactive Human Resources (HR) analytics dashboard built with Microsoft Power BI. It analyzes a dataset of 1,000 employee records to explore workforce composition, departmental headcount, salary distribution, employee activity, and attrition.

## Business Problem

HR teams need reliable workforce information to understand employee demographics, compare departments, monitor salary expenditure, and assess employee retention.

This dashboard brings key workforce indicators into one place to support HR reporting and management decision-making.

## Project Objectives

- Monitor total employee headcount and active employees.
- Compare employee distribution across departments.
- Examine workforce composition by gender and age.
- Analyze salary expenditure by department and month.
- Track employee attrition using the available dataset and defined measures.

## Tools and Technologies

- Microsoft Power BI — dashboard design and data visualization.
- Microsoft Excel / CSV — dataset storage and preparation.
- DAX — KPI calculations and measures.

## Key Performance Indicators

- Total Employees
- Total Departments
- Active Employees
- Attrition Rate
- Total Salary
- Average Salary

## Dashboard Visualizations

- Employee distribution by gender.
- Employee distribution by age.
- Employee headcount by department.
- Total salary by department.
- Total employees compared with active employees.
- Monthly salary trends.

## Initial Observations

Based on the dashboard screenshot:

- The dataset contains 1,000 employee records across 8 departments.
- The dashboard reports 797 active employees.
- Marketing has the highest displayed employee count, at 137.
- Finance has the highest displayed departmental salary total, at approximately ₦75 million.
- The displayed workforce gender distribution is relatively balanced, with 507 female and 493 male employees.

These observations are based on the visible dashboard and should be validated against the underlying data and calculations.

## Business Recommendations

- Investigate differences in staffing levels across departments to identify potential workforce planning needs.
- Review departmental salary expenditure alongside headcount and job roles to understand cost drivers.
- Monitor employee activity and validate the attrition calculation before making retention decisions.
- Examine age and gender distributions to support fair and responsible workforce planning.
- Review monthly salary patterns to identify unusual changes and confirm whether they reflect payroll timing, staffing changes, or data quality issues.
